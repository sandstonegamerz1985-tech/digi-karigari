Demo product image credits
==========================

All people and listings are fictional prototype data. Each photo below was manually selected to show the same kind of item or craft as its listing. Commons photos are illustrative references and are not photos of these fictional sellers' actual inventory. Product 29 uses the locally bundled cushion-cover photo. Wikimedia images are used unchanged.

Product number ranges refer to the order in `PRODUCTS` in `backend/app/seed.py`. The seed refresh updates existing demo listings when the backend starts.
